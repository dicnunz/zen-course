Paper 3

Please choose one of the following questions to answer. Please type out the question you are answering at the top of your paper. The paper should be 1000–1200 words and is due during Session 18.

  1. Bielefeldt shows that Dōgen’s meditation manuals inherit earlier Chinese forms. Explain why this does not reduce Dōgen to mere borrowing, and identify one revision whose significance depends on the comparison.
  2. Explain practice-realization without turning it into either “practice causes enlightenment” or “practice is unnecessary because enlightenment is already present.” Defend your interpretation against the strongest objection.
  3. Choose either Genjōkōan or Uji. Reconstruct one philosophical claim from the text in clear prose, then show what is lost by a familiar but weaker interpretation.
  4. Dōgen is often contrasted with koan-centered Rinzai. Using Dōgen’s actual use of koan literature, argue for a more precise distinction between his approach and a later Rinzai curriculum.
