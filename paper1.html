Paper 1

Please choose one of the following questions to answer. Please type out the question you are answering at the top of your paper. The paper should be 1000–1200 words and is due during Session 8. The rewrite is due during Session 12.

  1. Reconstruct the strongest historical case for treating Chan patriarchal lineage as a legitimating institution rather than a transparent family tree. Does this make lineage religiously false? Defend a precise answer.
  2. Shenhui helped establish Huineng as Sixth Patriarch. Explain how doctrinal polemic and succession claims reinforce one another, then argue whether the historical construction of Huineng’s status undermines the teachings attributed to him.
  3. The Platform Sutra praises no-thought. Give an interpretation that does not collapse into mental blankness, state the textual reasons for it, and answer the strongest objection.
  4. “Ordinary mind is the Way” is sometimes read as permission to abandon disciplined practice. Reconstruct the best version of that reading and argue, from the Hongzhou material, whether it succeeds.
