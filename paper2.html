Paper 2

Please choose one of the following questions to answer. Please type out the question you are answering at the top of your paper. The paper should be 1000–1200 words and is due during Session 15.

  1. Encounter dialogues are often preserved in sources later than the masters they depict. Explain what can and cannot responsibly be inferred from them, then defend a method for using them without either credulity or wholesale dismissal.
  2. Welter argues that the received Record of Linji reflects Song-dynasty construction of Chan orthodoxy. Does a late textual formation weaken Linji’s authority for later Zen practice? Distinguish historical and normative authority.
  3. Koans are frequently described as devices for transcending language. Using the literary and curricular evidence, formulate the strongest version of that thesis and argue whether it is adequate.
  4. What exactly is Dahui’s objection to silent illumination? Give a charitable reconstruction of both Dahui and Hongzhi, then decide whether their disagreement is primarily about meditative mechanism, rhetoric, or lineage formation.
  5. Can sudden awakening coherently require gradual preparation and post-awakening cultivation? State the apparent contradiction, resolve it if possible, and identify what remains philosophically unsettled.
