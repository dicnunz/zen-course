Zen

This course is a historically and philosophically rigorous introduction to Zen as a changing family of Chan, Seon, and Zen texts, practices, lineages, and institutions. Readings move from early Chinese Chan through kōan literature, Song meditation debates, Dōgen and Japanese Zen, Rinzai training, ritual and ordination, and modern global formations. The emphasis is on reconstructing claims, testing interpretations against primary and historical evidence, and resisting both romantic essentialism and reflexive debunking.
