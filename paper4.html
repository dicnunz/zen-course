Paper 4

Please choose one of the following questions to answer. Please type out the question you are answering at the top of your paper. The paper should be 2000–2400 words and is due during Session 25.

  1. Is Hakuin’s model of initial breakthrough followed by extensive testing a solution to the epistemic problem of private religious experience, or does it merely transfer the problem to teacher authority? Defend a view.
  2. Are funerals, memorial rites, precepts, and merit practices peripheral accommodations around “real Zen,” or constitutive parts of Zen history? Build the best case for the view you reject before defending your answer.
  3. How should a lineage tradition evaluate equality? Use women’s ordination and authority in Sōtō Zen to distinguish doctrinal equality, institutional access, recognition, and power.
  4. What does the history of Zen and Japanese nationalism show about the claim that awakened insight is self-authenticating and politically reliable? Give a careful argument that avoids both guilt-by-association and claims of spiritual immunity.
  5. Choose one modern claim about the “essence of Zen” - pure experience, meditation, spontaneity, anti-ritualism, or being beyond words. Trace how the course complicates it historically, then argue whether a defensible core remains.
